Case Study 01
The Testimonial Hook
"5 weeks ago she said 'I have a holiday in 5 weeks'" — Transformation Post

Design Elements

🎯
Emotional Hook: Opens with her goal, not your service. Makes it relatable.
🔥
Bold Typography: Massive hot pink header pulls attention. High contrast against dark background.
📸
Transformation Proof: Side-by-side before/after. Visible abs and definition = credibility.
📊
Metrics Bar: "7 LBS DROPPED" in bold. Specific numbers build trust.

Copy Strategy

💬
Subheader: "This is what we did about it." Positions YOU as the problem-solver.
✅
Proof Points: "No crash diets. No skipping life. Just the right plan." Addresses fears.
🎯
CTA: 'DM me "HOLIDAY" to get started.' Specific action word. Creates low-friction engagement.
🚀
Closing Line: "Results like this don't need a year — they need the RIGHT COACH." Subconscious authority play.
WHY THIS WORKS
This post follows the "Problem-Solution-Result" framework. It starts with a relatable client problem (deadline for vacation), shows the emotional journey, proves the results with visual evidence, and ends with a low-friction CTA. The specific action ("DM 'HOLIDAY'") is way more effective than generic "Contact me" because it feels like joining a community, not being sold to.
Case Study 02
The Authority Builder
"I've coached 600+ men through a body transformation" — Social Proof Post

Design Elements

📍
Distressed Typography: Textured white lettering = credibility. Looks like a gym poster, not a pretty graphic.
🟠
Orange Accent Bar: Breaks up dark background. Guides eye to key phrase.
👤
Coach Portrait: Shows real person, not generic. Builds trust through authenticity.
💪
Gym Context: Dumbbell visible. Immediately establishes credibility as a real coach.

Psychological Strategy

🎯
Opens with Objection: "Here's what nobody wants to admit about why most of them struggled before coaching." Addresses the real fear.
🔓
Vulnerability Angle: Suggests most people struggle = the prospect isn't alone. Reduces shame.
🏆
Big Number: "600+" is high enough to be impressive, low enough to be believable. Not 10,000.
⭐
No CTA: This is pure authority building. Let it do its job without asking for immediate action.
WHY THIS WORKS
This graphic doesn't ask for anything. It's pure positioning. It acknowledges a common fear (struggling before coaching), positions the coach as someone who's seen it 600+ times, and uses raw authenticity (gym setting, real person) to build credibility. The distressed typography and gym aesthetic make it feel earned, not designed. Prospects who see this think "This person knows what they're doing" and remember it.
Case Study 03
The Comparison Hook
"Before" vs "After" Physique — Transformation Series

Design Elements

🎯
Big Header: "BART PHYSIQUE CHANGE" — massive, easy to read from mobile scroll.
🔲
Rounded Photo Frames: Softens the images. Makes it feel premium, not clinical.
🟠
Orange Labels: "BEFORE" vs "AFTER" in bold orange. Clear visual direction.
📋
Icon Row: 3 benefit icons at bottom (body fat, shape, photo-ready). Makes benefits scannable.

Conversion Mechanics

👀
Stop Power: Body transformation = highest engagement rate on fitness feeds. People stop to look.
📊
Metrics Clarity: 3 specific benefits listed. Prospects know exactly what to expect.
📱
Carousel Format: Design works as slide 1 of a series. "Swipe to see more" = built-in engagement.
✨
Aesthetic: Dark, premium look. Makes transformation feel like a big deal, not just vanity.
WHY THIS WORKS
Transformation content is the highest-converting content in fitness Instagram. This design maximizes that by being super clear (BEFORE/AFTER), adding visual interest (rounded frames, icon row), and making it easy to understand benefits at a glance. The carousel format means it shows up in feeds multiple times as people swipe, multiplying the exposure. Best used once per week to avoid audience fatigue.

FAQ

Questions about the work.

What about copyright/model releases?
I work only with coaches who have permission and model releases from clients. If using client photos, they're either cropped to hide faces or blurred for privacy. All graphics are 100% original designs, not templates.
Can you use my brand colors?
Yes. I'll match your existing brand colors, fonts, and aesthetic. Send me your brand guidelines and I'll incorporate them seamlessly while keeping designs high-performing.
What tools do you use?
Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator) + Canva Pro for quick designs. All files delivered in the format you need (PNG, JPG, PSD, or Canva editable links).
How long does the Retainer package take?
